Code for MatchaBot, which competed at the York University 2024 Robot Sumo Competition.

▶️ [Best Moments Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnpWhO1nUXE)

- Programmed the robot that got 1st Place at the 2024 York University Mini Robot Sumo Competition, using a modular approach that facilitated strategy changes between matches.
- Reduced noise readings by the IR sensor from 8% to 3%, and increased main loop speed by 178% while searching for the opponent (* in relation to previous robot, see EspressoBot).
- Developed a strategy that evaded the opponent and attacked it from the side, ensuring victory 100% of the times we enabled it.
- Won 15 out of 16 matches.
